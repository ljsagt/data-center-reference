Important But No Score Change

  • ABF substrate: Unimicron raised 2026 capex 58%, nearly doubled long-lead equipment spending, and expects shortages through 2029. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • HDI PCB: Price increases, richer mix, and better yield lifted Unimicron's gross margin sharply. Mix is expected to matter more than further hikes, so the row remains 5/4/4.
  • Optical transceiver: Corning's enterprise-network sales rose 65%, and InnoLight rejected a very low 1.6T price rumor. The broad row remains 4/4/4 because there was no new allocation or moat trigger.
  • LPDDR5X / SOCAMM: Supplier allocation covers only about 60% of Nvidia's requested volume, but Vera Rubin is cutting SOCAMM content. The row remains 5/5/3.
  • Power semiconductors: Rohm is joining Delta earlier in product development, but wide supplier and technology competition keeps the row at 5/5/4.
  • Glass core substrate: New entrants target late-2027 volume, but commercial yield and qualification remain unproven. The row stays 3/3/4.

Watchlist

  • Enterprise SSD / KV-cache offload: Kioxia expects enterprise SSD ASP to outpace NAND, has secured up to half of capacity under LTAs, and expects an XL-Flash qualification result for an Nvidia storage rack in September.
  • Optical photodetector chips: WIN Semiconductor pulled forward mass production as data-center optical demand lifted utilization to 65%; the scorecard has no standalone photodetector row.
  • Nearline HDD / magnetic media: Seagate reported nearline price per exabyte up 10.2% and data-center revenue up 57%. Resonac agreed a customer price revision while planning a 44% media-capacity increase.
  • EMIB-T silicon-bridge packaging: Expected shipments moved forward by one year as Rubin Ultra changes and custom-ASIC interest expand demand; current capacity remains constrained.
