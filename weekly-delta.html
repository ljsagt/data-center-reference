Pricing Power

  • pricingCapacitors (Film, Electrolytic, Ceramic): 3 → 4. Nichicon implemented selected price revisions while large AI-server capacitor demand continued to expand. Demand remained around plan, so bottleneck stayed at 3.

Moat

  • moatNo moat score changed. Glass-core manufacturing remains difficult despite delayed volume demand. DFB supply is tighter, but the evidence did not narrow the qualified laser set enough to lift moat. Broad capacitor competition keeps moat at 3.

Important But No Score Change

  • ABF package substrate: Ibiden says demand remains above capacity, price and mix drove its guidance increase, and meaningful capacity takes at least two years. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • MLCC: Taiyo Yuden's book-to-bill reached 1.72, its AI-server growth outlook rose to 150%, and selected distributor prices increased. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • Low-loss PCB material: Resonac expects almost fourfold AI-server CCL growth, while special-glass demand and pricing stayed strong. The broad row remains 5/5/4.
  • Power semiconductors: Infineon disclosed multi-year AI capacity reservations worth high-single-digit billions of euros. The broad row remains 5/5/4.
  • HBM: Citi expects shortages to worsen into 2027 and customers are pursuing three-to-five-year supply agreements. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • EML: The same InP and booking evidence confirms a row already at 5/5/5.

Watchlist

  • Low-CTE and low-Dk glass cloth: Strong demand, price revisions, and no large near-term capacity announcement; no standalone scored row.
  • InP laser substrates: A 7–10x capacity plan and possible large price resets; no standalone substrate row.
  • Optical fiber and cable: Large new investment could meet today's tight demand but ease supply around 2028.
  • Photonics SOI wafers: Multi-year capacity reservations and earlier 300 mm qualification; the table scores modulators, not SOI substrates.
  • Texas grid-connection audits: Large builds may slow, but this is project-level timing evidence.
  • Server CPU and custom ASIC capacity: Near-term CPU limits and a larger 2027-28 ramp remain outside the scored component set.
