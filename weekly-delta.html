Watchlist

  • Near-package optics and optical switching: NPO interest is rising as scale-up CPO slips toward 2029, while OCS, coherent-lite, and microVCSEL architectures are developing quickly; these do not have standalone rows.
  • Nearline HDD, heads, and media: Supply shortages may persist beyond 2029, contract durations remain short, and component suppliers are asking for more than 20% increases.
  • Generic DRAM, NAND, enterprise SSD, and server CPUs: Deep 2027–28 memory deficits and server demand above shipment capacity are supporting price increases outside the table's HBM and SOCAMM rows.
  • Raw silicon wafers: Ex-China supply may turn short in the second half of 2027, with meaningful new supply unlikely before 2029.
  • Complete BBU systems: A new sector report sizes the market near USD 50 billion by 2030 and puts Panasonic's current share at 70–80%; the table contains BBU parts, not a complete-system row.
  • Liquid-cooling systems and services: Suppliers are expanding module and CDU capacity, while Vertiv added fluid-management and load-testing services; these are assembly- or service-level signals.
  • Grid, permitting, and complete power systems: Texas permitting risk and an Oracle project notice show site-level friction but do not isolate a scored subcomponent.
