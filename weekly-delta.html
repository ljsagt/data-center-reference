Watchlist

  • Optical DSPs and CPO test equipment: DSPs are the immediate constraint in China optical checks, while CPO yield, alignment, and new 3D inspection remain difficult; neither concept has a standalone row.
  • Cold plates and liquid-cooling assemblies: New checks say demand will exceed supply through 2027 as Vera Rubin and custom ASICs move to liquid cooling; these assemblies do not have standalone rows.
  • Large power transformers: Hitachi Energy will more than double US capacity, but the new factory starts only in 2029. The evidence is system-level, not tied to one scored subcomponent.
  • Generic DRAM, NAND, and enterprise SSD: Data-center demand and contract prices remain strong, while limited NAND pockets are starting to ease and new capacity is planned.
  • Complete BBU systems: One sector initiation warns Panasonic's roughly 80% BBU share could halve as competitors enter. The table contains BBU parts, not a complete-system row.
  • Connectors, fiber, and complete PSUs: Rubin-era connector counts could triple, fiber demand is rising, and one PSU supplier still sees component constraints, but the evidence does not isolate a scored component.
