Important But No Score Change

  • ABF package substrate: A new expert check estimates a 30–50% ABF/T-glass gap, staged 8–10% price resets, and one-to-two-year qualification. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • EML: Lumentum says demand is more than 30% above supply and pump lasers are sold out; Coherent's 1.6T ramp is accelerating. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • Low-loss PCB material: High-speed glass may remain short for three years and AI PCB/CCL forecasts rose, but M8/M9/PTFE alternatives keep moat at 4. The row remains 5/5/4.
  • Power semiconductors: Lead times rose again while MOSFET and power prices increased 12.8% and 11.3%. The broad row remains 5/5/4.
  • HBM: New checks still show 2027–28 shortage conditions and firm pricing even after a lower Nvidia content assumption. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • External CPO lasers: Lumentum, Coherent, and Third Bridge describe different suppliers and ramp dates by architecture. The row stays 5/4/4 pending design-specific sourcing clarity.

Watchlist

  • Low-CTE and T-glass cloth: Nitto Boseki may add another 30% of capacity and needs large price increases to fund it; no standalone scored row.
  • InP laser substrates: JX targets roughly tenfold capacity and 50–60% long-run margins, while Coherent is moving more output to six-inch InP; no standalone substrate row.
  • Optical fiber and cable: Amphenol's datacom fiber business doubled and is approaching Corning's scale; no standalone scored row.
  • Server rails: King Slide's margins, patents, and server demand look strong, but 70% utilization and new capacity do not indicate a bottleneck.
  • Server power supplies and HVDC racks: Delta expects server-power sales to more than double, and Flex is building 400V/800V capability; the evidence is system-level.
  • Generic DRAM/NAND/HBF, CPO alignment and FAU, PCB drill bits, 300 mm silicon wafers, and neocloud deployment: These remain visible watch items because the table has no directly matching row or the evidence is project-level.
