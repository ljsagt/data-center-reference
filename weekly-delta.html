Important But No Score Change

  • HBM: New capacity still takes two-to-five years and multi-year contracts support demand. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • HDI PCB: Fine-line mSAP capacity could remain about 30% short in 2027. The row remains 5/4/4 because there was no fresh price reset or smaller qualified supplier set.
  • Low-loss laminate: Resonac described a 30% CCL price increase, long qualification, and operator-limited supply. The broad row remains 5/5/4 because next-generation material competition is still open.
  • EML: EML remains tighter than DSP supply and prices are rising. The row remains 5/5/5.
  • Power semiconductors: A 1 MW rack can add about 700 SiC devices and most current designs point to 800V. The row remains 5/5/4 because the broad supplier and technology set is still wide.

Watchlist

  • Two-phase liquid cooling: 200 kW-class racks increase the need, but greenfield economics, OEM warranties, and single-phase competition keep adoption at pilot scale.
  • High-end PCB drill bits: Rubin can use four bits per board, early yield is below 70%, and grinding-equipment lead time is about one year.
  • Electronic-grade PPO resin: M8 demand is firm, but SA9000 supply is already ample and new capacity can pressure price.
  • HBF and enterprise storage tiers: HBF can add much more model-weight capacity, but qualification and software push broad adoption toward 2030.
  • CPO manufacturing: High channel counts, FAU alignment, and wafer-level optical testing remain difficult.
  • 800V solid-state circuit breakers: Fast fault isolation and long qualification support 2027 adoption, but there is no architecture-specific scored row.
  • Neocloud deployment execution: Power, liquid cooling, rack, and data-center space are replacing GPU supply as the installation constraint.
