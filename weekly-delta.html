Watchlist

  • Photonics-SOI substrates: Soitec expects multi-year capacity reservations with about eight of its ten largest customers after a second guidance increase; no SOI substrate row exists.
  • Nearline HDD components: Head, media, test, glass-substrate and HAMR-transition limits constrain output through 2027-28; no HDD-component rows exist.
  • Generic DRAM, NAND, and enterprise SSD: Prices are rising and data-center memory supply is reported 20-30% below demand, while new capacity is also planned.
  • Cold plates and microchannel cooling: Auras may enter Nvidia's Vera Rubin cold-plate chain and TSMC is integrating microchannels into packaging; neither concept has a row.
  • Grid-level power systems: Land, power, transformers, switchgear, batteries, and complete power trains are gating deployments, but the evidence did not isolate an existing Tier-2 row.
  • Complete AI servers and manufacturing equipment: OEM purchase commitments surged while hybrid-bonding timing moved toward 2028; finished systems and tools are outside the table.
